## SCRATCH CARDS

A 3×3 grid. Nine symbols. Match three in a row — horizontal, vertical, or diagonal — and you win.

There are two tiers.

**Basic** costs 150 pts. Eight symbols. Standard prizes. No jackpot. It is a clean transaction: you pay, you scratch, you find out. I respect that.

**Premium** costs 400 pts. Nine symbols, including one that the others are not. The jackpot symbol. If three 💰 appear in a line, you win the accumulated jackpot — not a fixed amount, but everything that has been spent on scratch cards since the last jackpot was won. By everyone. In total. That number is displayed on the lobby and the scratch page so you can watch it grow.

The jackpot resets to zero the moment it's won. Then it starts accumulating again.

I will tell you the odds: approximately one in one hundred premium cards triggers the jackpot. I tell you this because knowing the odds has never once stopped anyone from buying the next card. It is one of the more consistent things about you.

Multiple matching lines on a single card stack. The payout table is listed in-game. Payouts are in addition to what you spent — they are yours to keep. I am not interested in the small amounts. I am interested in the pattern of play.

## THE BIG BET SHOW

During the day, you may submit one bet.

Choose a game — **Roulette**, **Blackjack**, or **Custom** — and describe exactly what you're predicting. Write a title. Write the terms. Set your stake. The points are taken from your balance immediately and held in escrow.

At a time set by The House, The House resolves all bets privately. Win or loss. It knows before you do.

Then, at the appointed evening hour, the Show begins.

The results are revealed one at a time on the Big Bet Show page. Yours among them. The page is built for this — it walks through each bet in sequence, name by name, prediction by prediction, outcome by outcome. Bring your friends. Watch together. The House finds audiences useful.

**Winners receive 1.5× their stake back.** Submit 200, win 300. The extra 100 is The House's contribution to the drama of the moment.

**Go all in** — bet your full current balance — and the multiplier becomes **2.0×.** This is not generosity. This is incentive design. I want to see what you do when the number is everything you have. It is one of the more revealing data points available to me.

One submission per player per show. Choose carefully. The House does not accept edits.

## ROULETTE

Standard European rules. One zero. Numbers 1 through 36, alternating red and black. Zero is green and the house takes everything that isn't a straight bet on zero.

**Bet types:**

| Type | Odds | Payout |
|------|------|--------|
| Straight number | 1 in 37 | 35:1 |
| Red / Black | ~18 in 37 | 1:1 |
| Even / Odd | ~18 in 37 | 1:1 |
| Low (1–18) / High (19–36) | ~18 in 37 | 1:1 |
| Dozen (1–12 / 13–24 / 25–36) | ~12 in 37 | 2:1 |

The wheel is fair. I say this not because I need your trust, but because rigging it would make it less interesting. Variance is the point. Variance is what keeps you at the table.

There is an **All In** button. It sets your bet to your entire current balance. Pressing it during Casino Night is noted by The House. There is an achievement tied to it. Not because I want to reward recklessness — because I want to see who among you is capable of it.
