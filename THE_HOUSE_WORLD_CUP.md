## § 0 — A NOTE FROM THE HOUSE

Every four years, the world stops to watch men kick a ball into a net. We've seen it many times. We find it efficient — so much happening, so many opinions formed, so many of those opinions expressed with more confidence than the evidence warrants.

You are one of those people. That is not a criticism. It is, in fact, exactly why we built this.

The World Cup Event is not a replacement for the rest of the platform. It is an addition. The wagers continue. The wheel spins. The bingo card sits there, waiting. Everything you already know still applies. This is simply another room in the building — and we've been saving it for this.

Read carefully. The event has more moving parts than usual, and we've found that players who understand the mechanics fare significantly better than players who guess. We are invested in your success. Understand that clearly — it is not the same as being on your side, but it is real, and it should motivate you to read this twice.

Participating in the World Cup Event costs **500 points**. This is not a stake. It does not come back if your team loses. It goes somewhere specific, and Section II will explain exactly where.

In exchange, you choose a team. One team. The one you believe in — or the one you are willing to pretend to believe in for the duration of the tournament. We don't need sincerity. We need commitment. There is a difference, and you already know which one matters here.

Once the bracket locks — which happens the moment the first match of the Round of 32 kicks off — picks and entries are sealed. There is no editing. There is no late registration. The window is exactly as wide as it looks, and then it closes.

Don't wait. We've watched players miss the window and we'd genuinely rather not watch you do the same. You have time right now. Use it.

Every entry fee — 500 points from each player, plus any proxy fees paid during the event — accumulates into a single shared pool. We hold it. We don't touch it. We just let it sit there and grow while the tournament runs.

When the tournament ends and a champion is crowned, every player who backed the winning team splits the pool equally.

```
  All entry fees + all proxy fees
  ──────────────────────────────  =  each winner's share
       Number of winners
```

Three things here that we want you to actually think about:

**First** — the fewer players who backed the champion, the larger your share. Backing a favourite is comfortable. Backing a longshot and being right is something else entirely. We won't tell you what to pick. But we think you already have a feeling about this tournament. Trust it.

**Second** — original allegiance takes priority. If you picked the winning team as your **first choice** at entry, the entire pool comes to you and any proxy backers of that team receive nothing. Proxies only split the pot if every single backer of the winning team arrived there via proxy — meaning nobody called it from the start. Call it from the start.

**Third** — proxy fees count regardless of who wins. Every time a player pays to back a new team after elimination, that 250 points goes into the pool. Even if they never see a return, their money is already in there, building yours. There will be more on that in Section VI.

The Confidence Wager is the event's primary financial instrument. Here's how it works:

You and another player each stake a number of points. You back your team. They back theirs. The teams play each other. The winner collects both stakes.

Simple in concept. We made it more interesting. You're welcome.

```
  ░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░
  ░                                                ░
  ░   N E P H I ' S   R U L E                     ░
  ░                                                ░
  ░   When the lower stake is less than half       ░
  ░   of the higher stake, the wager becomes       ░
  ░   asymmetric.                                  ░
  ░                                                ░
  ░   HIGH-STAKE player wins:                      ░
  ░     → receives both stakes                     ░
  ░     → plus a 33% bonus on the high stake       ░
  ░       (paid by the system, not the loser)      ░
  ░                                                ░
  ░   LOW-STAKE player wins:                       ░
  ░     → receives both stakes                     ░
  ░     → minus 33% of the high stake              ░
  ░       (disappears from the pot entirely)       ░
  ░                                                ░
  ░   Nephi designed it. We adopted it             ░
  ░   immediately. Some rules just make sense.     ░
  ░                                                ░
  ░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░
```

The logic: a large stake deserves protection against a small one. Committing 300 points against someone's 50 should not carry the same risk profile as a symmetric bet. Nephi's Rule corrects for that. Whether it corrects in your favour depends entirely on which side you choose to be on — and that choice is yours to make.

**Draws** — if the match ends level, both players get their stakes back. No record is created. It's the least interesting outcome available to you. We trust you to aim higher.

**The Fan Competition cut** — 10% of your net profit from every settled confidence wager goes into a separate pot. Section IX covers what happens to it. Short version: it can come back to you.

**One restriction:** Confidence wagers are only available while your original team is still in the tournament. If they've been eliminated, this table is closed. The proxy option — Section VI — gives you a path back to parlays, but not this. Choose your team well.

A parlay is a multi-leg wager. You propose it. Someone else accepts each leg individually.

Each leg is a single prediction about a single match outcome. You pick a team, you pick a stake tier. The available tiers:

```
  100 pts per leg  →  1 🥤 per accepted leg
  200 pts per leg  →  2 🥤 per accepted leg
  300 pts per leg  →  3 🥤 per accepted leg
```

The cans shown above come to you — the proposer — the moment a leg is accepted. Before the match. Before you're right or wrong. Getting someone to take the other side is a skill, and we're curious whether you have it.

If a leg lands correctly, additional cans are awarded at settlement. Both sides earn them.

You can build up to 5 legs. Hit 3 or more and an achievement unlocks. Hit all 5 and something considerably better happens. We want to see someone go 5 for 5. We haven't seen it yet — at least not here. Prove us wrong on that.

**Pick a proxy team** — pay **250 points** to redirect your allegiance to a team still alive in the tournament.
