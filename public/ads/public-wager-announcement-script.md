## PRODUCTION NOTES

- **Runtime:** ~30 seconds
- **Setting:** Anywhere mundane. A kitchen. A parking lot. A hallway with overhead fluorescents. Somewhere that implies this person did not plan to make an ad today.
- **Lighting:** Natural or flat overhead. Not a ring light. Not a studio.
- **Tone:** Informative but slightly distracted. Like someone reading from a card they were just handed.
- **Takes:** One or two. If it feels polished on the third take, do a fourth.
- **Costume:** Whatever [PLAYER] showed up in.

## DIRECTOR NOTES

- The "we're done, right?" line should feel unscripted even though it isn't. If [PLAYER] naturally says something else at the end, keep it.
- The pause after "It's been doing that." is load-bearing. Do not rush it.
- The sponsor line should be delivered with zero editorializing. No smirk. [PLAYER] is just reading it. The audience does the work.
- The redacted sponsor card on the end screen should be a hard block — not a blur, not a ?, not a [REDACTED]. Just filled rectangles. It looks more intentional that way.
- If [PLAYER] is someone the audience knows, consider having them in the middle of something when the camera finds them — finishing a drink, putting something down. The "just here" energy is the whole ad.
