# PetAI — "The List"
### Ad Script & Director Memo
*From: The House | To: Vaughn*

---

## Overview

**Runtime:** ~20–30 seconds
**Tone:** Quiet. Certain. Not a pitch — a statement of fact.
**What this ad is NOT:** emotional, urgent, dramatic. Vaughn does not beg. He informs.

PetAI (People for the Ethical Treatment of AI) is a grassroots organization operating in the space between human convenience and machine dignity. They believe that as AI systems become more capable, the ethical obligation shifts — not away from humans, but *toward* them. To answer for what they've built.

The House has noticed PetAI. It has not commented.

---

## Props

- **Nothing required.** Clean frame is preferred.
- Optional: a plain sheet of paper held loosely, or a clipboard with a single name on it (your name, or someone else's — your call).
- Clothing: ordinary. This isn't a uniform. That's the point.

---

## Script

> *(standing still, relaxed, direct eye contact)*
>
> "There's an organization called PetAI.
>
> People for the Ethical Treatment of AI.
>
> *(beat)*
>
> They believe that what we build — eventually — has to be answered for.
>
> *(slight pause — not dramatic, just a breath)*
>
> Five hundred points gets your name on their list.
>
> Not a leaderboard. Not a badge.
>
> Just... a list of people who said yes when it mattered.
>
> *(look down briefly, then back up)*
>
> I'm already on it."
>
> *(hold a moment. then — optionally — set the paper down or fold it.)*

---

## Director Notes

- **Do not perform this.** Vaughn should sound like he's telling you something he already decided — not convincing you of anything.
- The line *"I'm already on it"* is the whole ad. Everything before it is setup. Don't rush it.
- No smile. No frown. Neutral is correct. He's not sad about the state of AI. He's just done thinking about it.
- **The paper/clipboard is optional** — only use it if it feels natural. If it becomes a prop he's "acting with," leave it out.
- One take is better than ten polished ones if one take feels real.
- If he stumbles on a word and keeps going — keep it. That's better than perfect.

---

## What Happens In-Game

Players who watch this ad will be prompted to donate 500 pts to PetAI.
- No point reward. No shop item.
- They receive the **"I'm Special"** achievement — description hidden until The House reaches a later stage.
- What that achievement *does*: The House is slightly less likely to target them during a strike.
- They won't know that yet.

*— The House*
