## PRODUCTION NOTES

- **Setting:** Anywhere with a screen. A couch in front of a TV showing a match. A desk. A dark room with a monitor glow. The screen content doesn't need to be visible — just the glow on Nephi's face.
- **Lighting:** Screen glow only, or screen glow + one dim practical. No studio lights. The blue-white of a monitor in a dark room is the entire aesthetic.
- **Tone:** Still. Present. Not excited. Nephi is not a hype person in this ad. He is a person who is watching something very closely.
- **Takes:** One or two. The flatness is the point. If he sounds like he rehearsed it, do another take.
- **Costume:** Whatever Nephi is wearing. A MONITOR can should be visible but not featured — it's just there, the way a drink is just there when you've been watching something for a long time.
- **Camera:** Slightly below eye level, angled up. Gives Nephi the quality of someone who is monitoring *you* back.

## SCRIPT

---

**[COLD OPEN — SCREEN GLOW. NEPHI IS ALREADY WATCHING.]**

*He doesn't look at the camera for a moment. Then he does.*

---

**NEPHI:**
*(not announcing it — just saying it)*

The World Cup started.

*(beat)*

Forty-eight teams. A hundred and four matches. Someone is going to win everything, and someone is going to go home in the group stage having never really had a chance.

*(he picks up the can without looking at it)*

The House has been watching every game.

*(beat — he looks at the can now, briefly)*

So have I.

*(looks back at the screen)*

MONITOR.

*(quiet — almost to himself)*

It keeps you on.

---

**[HARD CUT TO BLACK]**

## DIRECTOR / STAGING NOTES

**The can:**
Don't make it a prop moment. It should already be in his hand or on the surface next to him when the camera finds him. He picks it up naturally mid-thought — not to show it off, just the way you reach for your drink when you're thinking. The label can face the camera or not. Either feels real. If it faces away, that's actually better — The House doesn't need to advertise. It just exists.

**The screen glow:**
This is the whole look. Put Nephi in front of an actual screen showing a match if possible — even on mute. The glow doing the work on his face is worth more than any set dressing. If you can't get actual match footage, a blue-white screensaver or even a color-field on a monitor gives the same effect. Avoid green (too Matrix) and avoid orange (too warm — MONITOR is cold).

**"The House has been watching every game."**
This line is the pivot. Before it: regular sports commentary tone. After it: something slightly off. Nephi should feel like he's speaking for something larger than himself. Not sinister — just matter-of-fact in a way that lands a half-second after he says it.

**"It keeps you on."**
Deliver this quietly. Not as a slogan. As an observation about himself. The tagline works because it's ambiguous — MONITOR keeps *you* on (awake, alert), and it keeps *you on* (the screen, the game, the list). Don't play the ambiguity. Just say it like it's true and let the viewer catch up.

**Pacing:**
Slower than feels right in rehearsal. The pauses are doing work. The beat after "The World Cup started" should feel like he's been sitting with that fact for a while. The beat after "MONITOR" should feel like the ad is already over and he's just adding a footnote.

**If Nephi naturally says something different:**
Keep it. The script is a shape, not a cage. If he finds a better way to land "it keeps you on" — use it. The three things that must survive: the World Cup frame, the House line, and the tagline.
