## PRODUCTION NOTES

- **Setting:** Somewhere casual and lived-in. A couch. A kitchen counter. Somewhere Marcel actually spends time — not staged, just real. A phone or laptop visible nearby, ideally showing the Friendly Stakes app.
- **Lighting:** Natural or practical. Marcel is the approachable entry point — the person who explains it to you like a friend.
- **Tone:** Genuine excitement without being a pitch. Marcel knows something good is happening and wants you to know too. Not a salesperson. A person who's already in and thinks you should be.
- **Takes:** Let him talk. If he finds a better word than what's on the page, keep it. The script is a shape.
- **Costume:** Whatever Marcel is wearing. Energy drink can present, not featured.
- **Camera:** Eye level. Conversational. He's talking to the group, not performing for a lens.

---

## CLIP INSTRUCTIONS
*(For the editor — read before filming)*

This ad is designed to cut away to real World Cup footage at the two `[CLIP]` markers in the script below. Each clip should be **2–4 seconds**, dropped in as a hard cut and returned from cleanly.

**Where to find footage:**
- YouTube search: *"World Cup 2026 highlights"*, *"World Cup best goals"*, *"FIFA World Cup crowd reaction"*
- FIFA's official YouTube channel has historical highlight clips suitable for internal/private use
- For the first `[CLIP]`: a wide stadium shot or crowd erupting works well — something that says "this is actually happening"
- For the second `[CLIP]`: a goal celebration or a player's reaction right after scoring — something that lands with the same energy as "thirteen times"

**Editing notes:**
- Do not use clips mid-sentence. Each `[CLIP]` falls at a natural pause — hard cut out, hard cut back
- Slow-motion crowd footage is often more striking than raw goal footage for short inserts
- Portrait crop (9:16) if editing for mobile; landscape (16:9) for the in-app player
- Color grade the clips slightly cooler/darker if they look too "official broadcast" — you want them to feel like they belong next to Marcel, not like they're from a different show
- Keep the MONITOR can visible in Marcel's hand when you cut back from the second clip

## DIRECTOR / STAGING NOTES

**The phone:**
This is the object that grounds the scene. It should be there naturally, not as a prop. If he can actually have Friendly Stakes pulled up on it, great. If not, face-down is fine. The point is that he's someone who has already been in the app before this moment.

**"Five hundred points gets you in."**
Don't rush this. It should feel like a good deal, not a disclaimer. He's inviting, not selling.

**"Five legs — thirteen times."**
The pause before "thirteen times" is the beat. Marcel should land on that number like he's still a little impressed by it himself. Not performed surprise — just genuine. If he naturally says "five legs hits and you're getting thirteen times your money back," keep it.

**"Stay in it."**
 — this is encouragement, not observation. Marcel is rooting for you. The tagline should feel like something he'd actually say to a friend who was about to close the app.

**Monitor Cans:**
Not mentioned in the script by name. If Marcel naturally asks about them or brings them up, that's fine — but the ad doesn't need it. The event features carry the spot. The can is there for branding. Let it be a drink, not a mechanic.

**Pacing:**
Faster than Scott's but not rushed. Marcel has energy — use it. The pauses are still doing work, especially after "five legs" and after "MONITOR." Don't let the whole thing blur into one take.

**If Marcel naturally says something different:**
Keep it. The three things that must survive: the team pick, the parlay multiplier, and the tagline.
