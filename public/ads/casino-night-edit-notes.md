# CASINO NIGHT AD — VIDEO EDIT SUGGESTIONS

## Overall Structure

The ad has three acts. Each one should feel different in the edit:

1. **The Games** (fast, punchy, energy building) — 0:00–0:20
2. **The Casino Floor** (scratch cards, slots, roulette — midtempo, each one gets a moment) — 0:20–0:40
3. **Strike It Rich** (slow, heavy, dramatic) — 0:40–end

---

## Cut Suggestions

**Opening title card** — hold for 2–3 seconds max. If you have a subtle zoom or flicker effect, use it here. The House eye (👁) should feel like it's watching.

**CJ's entrance** — hard cut, no fade. He should already be in position when we see him. The sunglasses are already on. Don't show him putting them on — he lives in them.

**Game callouts (Tap Forge, Paint Shop, Echolocate)** — consider quick screen recordings or screenshots as B-roll overlays while CJ talks. Even 1-2 second flashes of the actual game UI give credibility. If no B-roll, keep the camera tight on CJ and let his energy carry it. Quick cuts between each game name if you have multiple angles.

**"I do not enjoy the games" beat** — hold the shot. No cuts here. Let the pause breathe. The comedy is in CJ's face during the silence. If he breaks even slightly, keep it.

**Score Sender section** — this is CJ pitching to himself. If you have a second angle, cut to it on "you earn points while you sleep." The shift from hype-man to genuinely interested should be visible.

**Scratch cards** — if possible, a screen recording of someone scratching a card in the app would be incredible B-roll here. Even a 3-second clip of tiles revealing. The jackpot pool number growing is also a great visual if you can capture it.

**Slots** — same idea. A quick clip of reels spinning, even from a phone screen capture, sells it instantly.

**"NONE of that is the main event"** — this is the pivot. Hard cut. Maybe a beat of silence before CJ starts the Strike It Rich section. If you're using music, this is where it drops or shifts.

**"FIVE. TIMES."** — if you do any text overlay in the whole video, do it here. Big, centered, maybe with a slight shake or pulse. "×5" filling the screen for half a second.

**"One player. One game. One shot."** — three cuts. One per phrase. Different angle or tighter zoom on each if possible. This is the ring announcer moment.

**Sunglasses back on** — let this be the last thing we see of CJ before the cut to black. Hold for a beat after he puts them on.

**End card** — match the opening card style. Hold for 3-4 seconds. If there's music, let it resolve here.

---

## Sound & Music

- **First half**: upbeat, lounge-y, builds energy. Something with a bassline that says "casino floor."
- **Transition to Strike It Rich**: music drops out or strips down to just bass/drums. The stakes are changing and the audio should reflect that.
- **"Five times" moment**: consider a beat drop or a single bass hit. Or total silence — both work.
- **"Don't keep The House waiting"**: music fades or cuts. This line should be the last thing you hear before the end card.
- If you want a sound effect anywhere, a single poker chip click on the cut to the end card is chef's kiss.

---

## Text Overlays (optional, sparingly)

Only use these if they add punch. Less is more:

- **×5** on "Five times your money" — big, centered, 0.5 seconds
- **Strike It Rich** when CJ first says the name — stylized, maybe with a glow
- **The payout numbers** (900 pts, 1800 pts, 50× bet) as quick flashes during the scratch card/slots sections — helps the numbers land

Do NOT overlay the game names during CJ's callouts — his delivery is the visual there.

---

## Color & Grade

- Lean warm and slightly desaturated for CJ's shots — casino lounge feel
- If CJ actually set up colored lighting (purple/red/gold), don't correct it. Let it look like a casino.
- The title cards should feel colder — dark, minimal, The House aesthetic. Contrast with CJ's warmth.

---

## Length Target

Aim for 55–70 seconds final. The script reads around 60 seconds at a natural pace, but CJ will add beats, pauses, and improvised lines. Cut anything that drags but keep every moment that's funnier or more intense than written. If the final cut is 75 seconds because CJ was on fire, that's fine — don't trim gold to hit a number.

---

## One Last Thing

If CJ nails the "I do not enjoy the games" → score sender → "only game I'm interested in" run and you can see him genuinely amused by his own bit — that's the thumbnail. Sunglasses half-down, mid-smirk, casino lighting. That frame sells the whole video.
