CHALLENGES
────────────────────────────────────────

🫵  Inspiring Friend — passive (next challenge pays 2x or 3x)
    Have 10 of your created challenges accepted by other players.
    Triggered automatically when the 10th acceptance is recorded.
    On unlock: if you already have a pending challenge out, it immediately
    becomes 3x. If not, your next created challenge gets 2x automatically.
    The multiplier is consumed when the challenge is posted — then resets to 1x.

👑  Baron — passive (collect 15% tax on all future challenge payouts)
    Have 20 of your created challenges accepted by other players.
    Triggered automatically when the 20th acceptance is recorded.
    On unlock: every time any challenge in the game settles as "completed",
    15% of the payout is taken from the winner and split equally among all
    active Baron holders (excluding the winner themselves).
    Multiple Barons split the pool. The tax scales with the payout size.

MONITOR CANS — EVENT CURRENCY REFERENCE
────────────────────────────────────────

Cans are earned during the World Cup event and stored on each player's
WorldCupEntry record. They are not yet spendable — event shop TBD.

Ways to earn cans:
  · Your team wins a match            → +3 cans (automatic, every win)
  · Opponent accepts a parlay leg     → cans per leg based on cost tier
      100 pt/leg = 1 can per accepted leg
      200 pt/leg = 2 cans per accepted leg
      300 pt/leg = 3 cans per accepted leg
  · Proposer: successful leg at vote  → same rate as above, per hit leg
  · Three-Leg Machine achievement     → +4 bonus cans (one-time)
  · Clean Sweep achievement           → +10 bonus cans (one-time)
  · Group Stage Prophet achievement   → +3 bonus cans (one-time)
