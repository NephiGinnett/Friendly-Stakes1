### PROPS (~$10 budget)

Purchase the following:
- **One box of Sysco-branded food service supplies** (paper towels, napkins, or a ketchup packet sleeve — whatever is at the restaurant supply store or Costco). The Sysco logo must be visible on camera. This is non-negotiable.

---

### SETUP & ANGLES

**Location:** Anywhere with a plain wall behind you. Kitchen, garage, a parking lot. Somewhere slightly institutional. Do NOT film in your bedroom.

**Angle 1 — WIDE SHOT:** Kyle standing, holding the Sysco box at chest level. Slightly too formal for the setting. The box should be readable.

**Angle 2 — MEDIUM SHOT:** Kyle looking directly into the camera, very serious. The clipboard, if obtained, should be visible.

No ring light. Slightly flat lighting is funnier. You are a spokesperson, not an influencer.

---

### SCRIPT

"Fortunately, I had Sysco Brand Security Alerts."

"From Sysco — the people who bring food to restaurants — comes *total digital protection* for your Friendly Stakes account. Every day: a fresh Ward, automatically. And if anyone touches it? You'll know. Immediately. On Discord."

*(nods very seriously)*

"I'm Kyle. I was almost a victim. Now I'm a subscriber."

*(looks at box, looks back at camera)*

"Sysco. They do food. And now... *this.*"

### DIRECTOR NOTES

- Do not smile during the "I was almost a victim" line. Commit.
- The line "They do food. And now... *this.*" should have a half-second pause before "*this.*" and a slight look of disbelief — like even Kyle is not fully sure this is real.
- One clean take is ideal. Two takes maximum. Any more and it loses sincerity.
- Landscape orientation. 1080p if possible.
