### How a Wager Works

There are two kinds of wagers. The House recommends understanding both before committing to either.

**Private Wagers** are closed tables. The Creator invites specific players. The pool is exactly what the participants bring — no more. No crowd. No bots. No House.

**Public Wagers** are open tables. Anyone may sit down. The House also sits down. The public weighs in. We encourage it.

In both cases, the Creator defines:

- The **Title** — the statement being bet on
- Their **Stake** — points committed to the FOR side
- The **Deadline** — when betting closes and settlement begins

The Creator always bets FOR. Everyone who joins chooses: **FOR** or **AGAINST**. The disagreement is the point. The House finds disagreement productive.

Once committed, your stake does not come back unless the wager is cancelled. There are no refunds. There are no do-overs. People have tried, bless them. It is a rule for a reason.

### Private Wagers — Pool Split

In a private wager, every point staked goes into a shared pool. Winners split it proportionally based on how much they staked.

```
  Your Stake
  ──────────  ×  Total Pool  =  Your Payout
  All Winner Stakes
```

Your share grows with your stake. Your share shrinks as other winners join your side. What you get depends entirely on who else bet with you and who bet against you.

### Public Wagers — Fixed Odds

Public wagers work differently. The system calculates your **locked payout** at the moment you join, based on the current odds. That amount is locked in — it does not change when new players arrive.

```
  Your Payout  =  Your Stake  +  floor( Your Stake / Your Side's Pool × Opposing Pool )
```

Before you place your bet, the page shows you the current odds and your expected payout for the stake you've entered. What you see is what you get if your side wins. The public absorbs any spread.

```
  ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │  Current odds       3:1                       │
  │  If you win         1,500 pts                 │
  │  Net profit         +1,000 pts                │
  └──────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**The Creator is the exception.** The Creator opened the table without knowing what the final odds would be — so their payout is recalculated at close, using the complete final pool. The risk of going first carries its own reward. The House believes this is fair. It designed it this way.

**All locked payouts are guaranteed in full.** The public backing a wager absorbs any shortfall. This is by design.
